Security Researcher

Huyen Tran

PhD Student at Chair of System Engineering — Dresden University of Technology

I'm a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of confidential computing, distributed systems, and systems security.

My work focuses on designing secure and trustworthy computing infrastructures, including policy-based management, key management, and protection mechanisms for confidential cloud-native applications. Driven by the belief that strong security foundations require both rigorous research and practical engineering, I explore solutions that bridge theoretical concepts with real-world system design and deployment.

Open Topics — Research Interests
  • Distributed Systems — Building secure, scalable, and resilient distributed systems with a focus on policy consistency, trust establishment, and cloud-native orchestration.
  • Confidential Computing & Zero Trust Architecture — Designing secure systems based on Zero Trust principles, leveraging Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs) to protect applications, data, and infrastructure in cloud-native environments.
  • Policy-Based Security Management — Developing policy engines and runtime enforcement mechanisms for secure authorization, access control, and automated policy lifecycle management in distributed systems.
  • Key Management — Secure key provisioning, distribution, rotation, and lifecycle management for confidential computing and distributed applications.
  • Rollback Attack Prevention — Designing mechanisms to detect and prevent rollback attacks on stateful security policies and confidential computing workloads without relying on trusted hardware counters.
